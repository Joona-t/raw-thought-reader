ABOUT THIS EDITION

A place to read,
and to keep reading.

This independent reader presents Aaron Swartz’s writing from the Raw Thought archive preserved by Josh Leitzel. These 449 posts span May 2005 to November 2012; this is the collection in that archive, not every post Aaron ever wrote.

The posts run from oldest to newest. Posts on the same day use archive filename order because publication times are not included. Original source links accompany every entry. Old scripts, subscription forms, and embedded plugins have been removed; archived images are available through links.

Your saved posts and last-opened article stay in this browser. No account, analytics, or third-party scripts. This site is an independent tribute, unaffiliated with Aaron’s estate or the original archive maintainer.
